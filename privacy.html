Jack Poster Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Jack Poster is a private tool run by NetEng LLC. It sends videos made by NetEng LLC to the Storytime with Jack TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public.

What it uses

When the account owner authorizes it, Jack Poster receives from TikTok an access token, an account ID and basic profile information (display name and avatar) for that one account.

How it is used

This information is used only to upload drafts to that account and to confirm upload status. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for advertising.

Storage

Tokens are stored on a computer controlled by NetEng LLC. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings, and tokens are deleted when the tool is no longer used.

Children

Jack Poster does not collect information from children or from viewers of the videos. It only handles information about the NetEng LLC account that authorized it.
